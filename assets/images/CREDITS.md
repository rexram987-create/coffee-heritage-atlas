## Notes

- The Arabica image was renamed for consistent project file naming; no visual editing was recorded at the time of upload.

## Usage rules

- Confirm the license on the original source page before adding an image.
- Prefer public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA material.
- Keep the original source-page URL, not only the direct image URL.
- Record required attribution exactly as requested by the license.
- Do not upload images with unclear ownership or editorial-only restrictions.
- Compress large images before publishing while preserving a high-quality original when useful.
